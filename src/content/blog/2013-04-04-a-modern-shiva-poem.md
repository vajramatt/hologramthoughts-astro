Lord Shiva, in meditation.

Namaste to He who is the speaker and the listener
Namaste to He who is the writer and the reader
Namaste to He who is the driver and the driven
Namaste to He who is the rain and the clouds
the flower and the seer of flowers
the apple and the tree.

Om Namah Shivaya
